NAP FIRMWARE MUSE CHO ROBOT BON (giu nguyen Wi-Fi, token va ghep doi)

Mo https://espressif.github.io/esptool-js/ bang Chrome, Baudrate 921600,
bam Connect, chon cong COM cua Bon. Them 4 dong (Add File) dung dia chi:

  0x0      1-bootloader-0x0.bin
  0x10000  2-partition-table-0x10000.bin
  0x17000  3-ota-data-0x17000.bin
  0x20000  4-bon-app-0x20000.bin

KHONG bam "Erase Flash". Bam Program. Xong rut dien cam lai.
Dat Bon nam phang tren ban luc bat (Bon do mat ban luc khoi dong).

Neu Bon khong ket noi lai duoc Muse: nap file merged-binary-XOA-HET-0x0.bin
o dia chi 0x0 (xoa het), roi vao trang web flasher cua Muse de gui lai
SDK token va ghep doi lai trong app Muse.

NAP KEY GOOGLE TTS (chi lam 1 lan, key luu trong Bon)
Arduino IDE > Tools > Serial Monitor, chon cong COM cua Bon, 115200 baud.
Tat Unikey (chuyen sang E). Dan 1 dong vao o go roi Enter:

  >tts_key=AIza...........

Moi dong anh go, Bon deu tra loi 1 dong bat dau bang "@":
  "@tts_key ok (39 ky tu)"  -> xong, Bon tu khoi dong lai.
  "@tts_key error ..."      -> key bi thieu/thua ky tu, dan lai.
  "@bon nhan duoc 1 dong"   -> Bon nghe thay nhung dong khong dung lenh.
  Khong thay gi            -> sai cong COM, hoac chua bam Enter.
Khong quay phim/chup man hinh luc go key.
Doi giong (tuy chon): >tts_voice=vi-VN-Neural2-D
